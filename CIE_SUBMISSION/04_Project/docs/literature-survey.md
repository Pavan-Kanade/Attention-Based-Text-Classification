# Literature Survey: Attention-Based Text Classification

## Surveyed Research Papers

### 1. Bahdanau et al. (2014)
- **Title:** Neural Machine Translation by Jointly Learning to Align and Translate
- **Authors:** Dzmitry Bahdanau, Kyunghyun Cho, Yoshua Bengio
- **Key Contribution:** Introduced Additive Attention to allow sequence decoders to search for relevant encoder hidden states dynamically.

### 2. Luong et al. (2015)
- **Title:** Effective Approaches to Attention-based Neural Machine Translation
- **Authors:** Minh-Thang Luong, Hieu Pham, Christopher D. Manning
- **Key Contribution:** Developed Global and Local attention mechanisms and simplified score calculation using matrix dot products.

### 3. Vaswani et al. (2017)
- **Title:** Attention Is All You Need
- **Authors:** Ashish Vaswani et al.
- **Key Contribution:** Replaced recurrent neural networks entirely with Scaled Dot-Product Self-Attention and Multi-Head Attention (Transformer).

### 4. Yang et al. (2016)
- **Title:** Hierarchical Attention Networks for Document Classification
- **Authors:** Zichao Yang et al.
- **Key Contribution:** Applied word and sentence level attention mechanisms to extract informative tokens for review classification.
